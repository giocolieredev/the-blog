Welcome to the blog. This is where I'll write about code, creativity, music,
and whatever experiments I'm running at the moment — short posts, no fluff.

<!--more-->

## What to expect

Mostly three kinds of posts:

1. **Dev notes** — things I built, bugs I hit, and decisions I made.
2. **Creative logs** — music, drawings, and design process.
3. **Experiments** — small toys and ideas that may or may not go anywhere.

The blog is a static site built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted
for free on GitHub Pages — same look and feel as the rest of [crii.me](https://crii.me).

## A tiny code sample

Here's the kind of thing you might see around here:

```js
function ship(idea) {
  return idea

}
```

## Why a blog?

> Writing is thinking in public. A blog is a nice excuse to finish ideas.
